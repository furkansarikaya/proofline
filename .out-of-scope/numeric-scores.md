# Numeric scores presented as facts

**Status:** Rejected.

No numeric confidence score for a run, no prioritisation score (reach times
impact style) for product opportunities, no numeric design quality score.

## Why rejected

A number invented by a model looks like a measurement and is not one. It
invites false precision and hides the reasoning.

## Instead

Qualitative labels with reasons (high, medium, low), ranked lists with a
one-line rationale per item, and real numbers only when a script computed
them from a recorded source. Estimates are allowed in assumptions when they
are marked as estimates with a basis.
