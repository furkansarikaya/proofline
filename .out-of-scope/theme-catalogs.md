# Theme catalogs and design skins

**Status:** Rejected, with a recorded exception.

proofline does not pick a ready-made visual theme or palette preset and
dress a product in it.

## The exception

ui-design vendors a library of composition, typography and motion decisions
derived from real sites, under its license notice. It is a source of
options, not a theme: a direction drawn from it must pass the subject
inventory, the idea sentence and the rival test, and is adapted to the
product.

## Not acceptable

- Shipping a direction because it is "clean and modern" or because it is in
  the library.
- Adding preset palettes or skins that bypass the idea sentence.
