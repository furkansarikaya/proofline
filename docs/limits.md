# What proofline does not do, and why that is safe

This page lists the limits the project has accepted on purpose, one row each, with the reason the limit is safe and the decision that recorded it. It is for users who want to know where the guarantees stop and for contributors who are tempted to "fix" one of these.

Each row names a decision (`A<n>` in [decisions.md](decisions.md)); the decision holds the evidence and the rejected alternatives. The page follows the project's own rule: no evidence, no claim. What the measured facts do and do not prove is on [proof.md](proof.md). A bold id such as A322.1 is the number of the item inside that decision record (A322 and A323 collect the limits of the end-to-end campaign and of the test and CI work that followed it, A324 those of the final pass, A338 those of the closing campaign of 2026-11-18).
